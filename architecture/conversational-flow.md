# Conversational Flow

## Overview

The conversational flow is designed to guide customers through different insurance services while maintaining context throughout the interaction.

## Flow

Customer

↓

Authentication

↓

Intent Identification

↓

Route to Appropriate Service

↓

Backend API Call

↓

Generate Response

↓

Continue Conversation

↓

End Conversation

OR

Human Escalation

## Supported Services

- Policy Inquiry
- Benefits Information
- Claim Status
- New Claim Initiation
- Policy Renewal
- Customer Onboarding
- Customer Update Requests
- Human Escalation
