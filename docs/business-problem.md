# Business Problem

Insurance companies receive a large number of repetitive customer queries related to policy information, claims, renewals, and customer updates. Handling these requests manually increases response time and operational costs.

The objective of this project is to automate these common customer interactions using a conversational AI voice agent while ensuring secure authentication and seamless escalation to human agents whenever required.

## Expected Benefits

- Faster customer support
- Reduced operational workload
- Improved customer experience
- Secure access to customer information
- Consistent responses
